Everything a dairy collection center needs

Six focused modules that cover the daily milk collection workflow from farmer to final payment.

Farmer Management

Add farmers with unique IDs, village and contact details. Search instantly and deactivate farmers who stop supplying.

Daily Milk Collection

Record morning and evening collections per farmer with liters, fat and SNF quality parameters for cow and buffalo milk.

Milk Rate & Payment

Set per-liter rates for cow and buffalo milk with effective dates. Every entry stores its own rate, so payments never change retroactively.

Collection History

Browse every past entry with filters for date range, shift, milk type and farmer. Totals for liters and amount are always in view.

Reports

Daily summaries, month-wise farmer totals, custom date ranges and per-farmer statements - calculated straight from your data.

PDF & Excel Export

Download any report as a clean PDF, or export full collection data to Excel for accounting, sharing and backup.
